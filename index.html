Tournament Format

*Format will be determined by the number of teams registered*

🔥 8-Team Format

  • 2 groups of 4 (3 qualifying games per team)
  • Games to 15 points, 25-minute limit
  • 5-point rally tiebreaker (first to 5)

💪 10-Team Format

  • 2 groups of 5 (4 qualifying games per team)
  • Games to 11 points, 15-minute limit
  • Tiebreaker applies (first to 5)

🥉 Semi-Finals

Group 1 #1 vs Group 2 #2 | Group 2 #1 vs Group 1 #2

Games to 15 — 25-minute limit.

🏆 Finals

Winners of semi-finals face off.

Game to 15 — last team standing wins! 😉
